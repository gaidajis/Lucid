## 2024-03-24 - Accessible Card Interactive Elements
**Learning:** When turning non-interactive elements (like `motion.div` wrappers used for layout/animation) into clickable items via `onClick` handlers, they implicitly lose all accessibility semantics. Specifically, the cards in `DataCard.tsx` were acting as buttons to open a modal but lacked keyboard navigation and screen reader support.
**Action:** Always ensure that `onClick` handlers on `div` or `motion.div` elements are accompanied by `role="button"`, `tabIndex={0}`, an `onKeyDown` handler (supporting 'Enter' and 'Space'), and clear `focus-visible` styling (respecting the theme, e.g., `focus-visible:ring-black dark:focus-visible:ring-white`) to preserve the expected interactive experience for all users. Furthermore, when dynamic states (like `editMode`) remove the interactive behavior, explicitly remove these accessibility attributes (`role={undefined}`, `tabIndex={undefined}`) and focus styles so users aren't confused by disabled or non-functional interactive elements.
